কৃষক বাজার দর - Bengali Mandi ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free Bengali-language agricultural market price app developed by Coreloom. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

কৃষক বাজার দর - Bengali Mandi does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. No account or login is required. All market price data is fetched from India's public government data portal (data.gov.in / agmarknet.gov.in) and displayed on your device.

Advertising

This app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and information about your interactions with ads in order to serve personalised advertisements. This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can review Google's data practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of personalised ads on Android, go to: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Notifications and Alerts

If you enable price alerts, the app stores your alert preferences locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to any server.

Offline Data Storage

The app may store recently fetched market prices on your device so they remain accessible without an internet connection. This data is stored only on your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The third-party services used in this app are:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • India Government Data Portal – agmarknet.gov.in (public agricultural price data)

We do not integrate any analytics or additional tracking services.

Children's Privacy

কৃষক বাজার দর - Bengali Mandi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the updated date at the top of this page.
